Tree of Heaven or Hell?

Data Visualization [2024]

By adopting an alternative perspective on biodiversity, the installation explores how digital platforms shape new forms of coexistence with the non-human world. The focus is on Ailanthus altissima (Tree of Heaven), a species admired for its resilience yet feared for its invasive nature. Tree of Heaven or Hell? analyzes discussions around Ailanthus across 76 YouTube videos and 925 comments, revealing how online discourse reflects broader power dynamics between humanity and nature.
The project features three artifacts: a supercut video introducing the context through escalating visual tension; a LED display looping selected comment fragments with an accompanying booklet; a catalogue and a network of miniatures representing elimination strategies suggested by users, visualizing how tools are combined within shared removal techniques.
