Lost&found

Service design, communication campaign [2023]

Lost&Found is an app supported by a physical and digital communication campaign, designed to stimulate the curiosity of Milan’s Generation Z and encourage city exploration.
The experience redefines getting lost as a positive and intentional act: by wandering without a predefined destination, users are invited to see the city with fresh eyes and make unexpected discoveries. After starting an exploratory session and choosing how to move around, the app generates random prompts that invite users to follow visual, olfactory, and sound stimuli. This experience can be shared with friends, fostering an active relationship with the urban environment and encouraging attention to details that might otherwise go unnoticed.
The communication strategy includes a poster system and a social media campaign to promote the experience. The entire service design process is documented in a project book, which presents the creative development and design choices supported by theoretical, technical, and literary references.
