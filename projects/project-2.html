Scintille

Editorial project [2024]

Scintille is an independent magazine born from the need to restore trust among Italian citizens towards Italian politics. Politics, especially among younger people, is often perceived as distant and difficult to engage with through dialogue and collaboration.
The goal of Scintille is to show how cooperation between citizens and politics is essential to addressing the major issues affecting the country and contributing to widespread distrust. The first issue focuses on natural disasters in Italy, whose consequences are often seen as inevitable and therefore met with resignation.
Through positive examples from Italy’s history, Scintille aims to demonstrate that dialogue with institutions is possible. These are the “sparks” (hence the magazine’s name in Italian) from which a renewed spirit of political participation can emerge. Revisiting the newspaper format, traditionally dedicated to current affairs and political news, with a fresh yet serious perspective offers a new way of engaging with these topics.
