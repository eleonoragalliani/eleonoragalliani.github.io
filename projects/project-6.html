.img: The Persistence of Ephemeral

Editorial project [2024]

With the rise of the web and digitalization, image archives have evolved from simple repositories of memories into structured datasets, turning images into data. This idea is at the core of .img — The Persistence of the Ephemeral: From Images to Data, an editorial project that explores the role of digital images not only as representations, but as carriers of memory and information. The .img suffix recalls a file format and symbolizes images as extensions of memory.
The project highlights a central paradox: digital images can make fleeting moments permanent by converting them into lasting data, yet they remain fragile, subject to technological obsolescence and the instability of the media that store them. The choice of a printed format is both conceptual and stylistic, bringing the digital image back into the analog realm while preserving the traces it leaves behind: its metadata.
