Gen—Zine

Editorial project [2024]

Gen-zine is designed to decode and explain the ever-evolving language of Generation Z, a generation shaped by digital culture and social media. Created in a constantly changing context, the first issue humorously explores some of the most commonly used slang expressions, going beyond simple definitions. It highlights the role of language as a tool for personal expression and cultural identity, showing how these terms reflect the experiences, values, and challenges of Gen Z. The tone of voice is authentic and friendly, engaging readers by mirroring current linguistic and cultural trends and fostering a connection that feels like a conversation among friends.
