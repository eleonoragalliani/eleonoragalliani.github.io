TEDxCourmayeur

Brand identity [2023]

This project involved designing the visual identity for the TEDx event held in Courmayeur in July 2023. Based on the theme Composition and Decomposition, a visual language was developed to support the narrative of the talks.
Starting from the idea that everything can be broken down into simpler forms and recomposed into more complex systems, the identity draws on the most fundamental units of the cosmos: the Platonic solids: the tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron, and icosahedron. These forms symbolize transformation while preserving the properties of the original structures, remaining independent yet part of a whole.
In the same way, TEDx presents a constellation of ideas that retain their individuality while contributing to a shared framework.
