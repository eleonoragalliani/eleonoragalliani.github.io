Master thesis [2026]

Designing access to the latent space is a thesis that investigates the concept of latent space in the context of generative artificial intelligence and its relevance to communication design. Starting from the changing relationship between designer, generative system, and output, the research considers latent space not only as a technical concept in machine learning, but also as a space of possibilities made accessible through visual, interactive, discursive, and experiential forms of mediation. The research question examines how latent space can be interpreted as a design material and through which forms it is made understandable, explorable, or practicable. It also asks how access to this space can be designed without reducing it to a promise of complete transparency or control.
