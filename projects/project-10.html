U-TURN

UX UI design [2024]

Developed during an Interaction Design course focused on togetherness and inspiration, U-TURN is an app designed to help users overcome the fear that it is too late to start a new educational or professional path. The hourglass serves as the core visual element, symbolizing that personal growth is defined by skills and progress rather than time.
Aimed at beginners approaching new skills or activities, the app addresses common challenges such as time management and comparison with others. Based on user research and interviews, U-TURN tracks progress through completed tasks rather than hours spent, combining quantitative indicators with qualitative feedback on emotional state.
The app recognizes and organizes newly acquired skills, suggests personalized content, and fosters a sense of community by connecting users on similar or complementary paths. AI support, powered by advanced language models, assists with writing and tailored recommendations, adapting to each user’s goals and needs.
