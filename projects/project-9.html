A Occhio Nudo

Editorial project [2022]

A Occhio Nudo is an editorial project conceived to reinterpret a literary text from a transmedia perspective. After selecting and analyzing Blindness by José Saramago, the narrative is rewritten from the point of view of blindness itself, personified as a conscious entity whose ironic voice guides the reader through the story.
The text is enhanced through graphic and typographic experimentation that evokes its unsettling content by engaging the only sense absent from the narrative: sight. As a result, readers are not only led through multiple narrative layers but are also physically guided across pages that simulate the suffocating and claustrophobic sensations of the events described.
